凝眸・Gaze 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新日期 / Last Updated: 2026-05-21

中文

凝眸・Gaze（以下简称“本应用”）非常重视用户隐私与数据安全。本隐私政策用于说明本应用如何收集、使用、存储、共享和保护您在使用本应用过程中产生的相关信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

在您使用本应用进行前庭性眩晕康复训练与评估时，我们可能会收集以下信息：

  • 前置 TrueDepth 摄像头和人脸追踪能力生成的训练相关测量数据。
  • 眼动方向、注视相关坐标、头动与头部姿态信息、眨眼相关数值。
  • 训练完成情况、训练表现、训练记录及恢复进度相关数据。
  • 问卷填写结果、症状评估结果及训练安排信息。
  • 应用为识别训练记录生成的用户标识（User ID）。

2. 摄像头与面部追踪数据

本应用使用设备前置 TrueDepth 摄像头及 Apple TrueDepth API，在训练过程中采集与人脸追踪相关的测量数据。这些数据可能包括眼动方向、注视相关坐标、头动与头部姿态信息、眨眼相关数值，以及由训练过程生成的时间戳和训练表现测量结果。

本应用不会将上述面部相关数据用于广告、营销、用户画像、跨应用追踪、人脸识别或身份验证。

本应用不以识别个人身份为目的收集或使用面部数据。

本应用不会保存原始 TrueDepth 相机图像、原始深度图或以图像/视频形式保存面部网格数据；本应用保存和处理的是用于康复训练与评估的派生测量数据与训练记录。

3. 我们如何使用这些信息

我们收集和处理上述信息，仅用于以下目的：

  • 提供前庭性眩晕康复训练功能。
  • 支持眼动、头动及相关训练模块的交互与反馈。
  • 评估您的训练表现、康复进度和当前状况。
  • 生成训练记录并支持后续康复效果分析。
  • 用于模型构建、优化和后续用户康复进度评估。
  • 改进本应用的训练方案、评估能力和产品体验。
  • 用于与本毕业设计相关的教学、研究与学术分析。

4. 数据上传、存储与保留

您在训练和评估过程中产生的训练记录及面部追踪相关测量数据，可能会以训练记录文件的形式暂时保存在设备本地，也可能上传至开发者专用服务器 https://www.gazeat.top，用于训练记录存储、恢复评估、研究分析及模型开发。

我们仅在实现本隐私政策所述康复训练、评估、研究与分析目的所合理必要的期限内保留相关数据，除非适用法律要求更长的保留期限。

5. 数据共享

我们不会出售面部数据，也不会与广告商或无关第三方共享面部数据。

除为实现本应用核心功能所必需的服务器处理外，我们不会向无关第三方出售、出租或随意披露您的个人数据。若法律法规另有要求，或为履行法定义务，我们可能在法律允许范围内披露必要信息。

6. 数据安全

我们重视您的数据安全，并采取合理的技术和管理措施保护数据免遭未经授权的访问、使用或泄露。但请您理解，任何互联网传输和电子存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。

如果您希望了解、更正或删除与您相关的数据，或对本隐私政策有任何疑问，您可以通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

8. 隐私政策更新

我们可能根据产品功能调整、法律法规变化或服务优化需要更新本隐私政策。更新后的版本将在适当位置公布。

English

Gaze (the “App”) values your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information generated during your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App for vestibular dizziness rehabilitation training and assessment, we may collect:

  • Training-related measurement data generated through the front TrueDepth camera and face-tracking capabilities.
  • Eye movement direction, gaze-related coordinates, head movement and head pose information, and blink-related values.
  • Training performance, completion records, and recovery progress data.
  • Questionnaire responses, symptom assessment results, and training plan information.
  • A generated user identifier (User ID) used to associate training records.

2. Camera and Face-Tracking Data

The App uses the device’s front TrueDepth camera and Apple’s TrueDepth API to collect face-tracking related measurement data during training sessions. This data may include eye movement direction, gaze-related coordinates, head movement and head pose information, blink-related values, and timestamps and training performance measurements generated during the training process.

The App does not use face-related data for advertising, marketing, profiling, cross-app tracking, facial recognition, or identity verification.

The App does not collect or use face data for the purpose of identifying a person.

The App does not store raw TrueDepth camera images, raw depth maps, or face mesh data as images or videos. The App stores and processes derived measurement data and training records for rehabilitation training and assessment purposes.

3. How We Use This Information

We collect and process this information only for the following purposes:

  • To provide vestibular dizziness rehabilitation training functions.
  • To support interaction and feedback in eye-movement, head-movement, and related training modules.
  • To evaluate your training performance, recovery progress, and current condition.
  • To generate training records and support rehabilitation-effect analysis.
  • To support model development, optimization, and future recovery assessment.
  • To improve the App’s training plans, evaluation capabilities, and user experience.
  • To support education, research, and academic analysis related to this graduation project.

4. Data Upload, Storage, and Retention

Training records and face-tracking related measurement data generated during training and assessment may be temporarily stored locally on the device as training record files and may also be uploaded to the developer’s dedicated server at https://www.gazeat.top for storage, rehabilitation progress evaluation, research analysis, and model development.

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the rehabilitation training, assessment, research, and analysis purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell face data and do not share face data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Except for server-side processing necessary to provide the App’s core functionality, we do not sell, rent, or arbitrarily disclose your personal data to unrelated third parties. We may disclose necessary information where required by applicable law or legal obligation.

6. Data Security

We value data security and take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Rights

If you would like to access, correct, or delete data related to you, or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us using the details below.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to feature changes, legal requirements, or service improvements. The updated version will be published in an appropriate location.
