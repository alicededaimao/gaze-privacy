English

Gaze is a research-oriented project focused on vestibular dizziness rehabilitation training and assessment. The app is used to collect eye-movement, head-movement, symptom questionnaire, and training-performance data during training sessions to support rehabilitation progress evaluation, training-effect analysis, and follow-up research.

This project is conducted in Professor Han Ting's laboratory at the School of Design, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with a focus on vestibular dizziness rehabilitation. If you encounter any issues while using the app, or if you have questions regarding training records, data usage, privacy, or support, please contact us using the information below.

Contact Email: annanicloe@foxmail.com
