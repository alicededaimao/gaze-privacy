中文

凝眸・Gaze（以下简称“本应用”）非常重视用户隐私与数据安全。本隐私政策用于说明本应用如何收集、使用、存储和保护您在使用本应用过程中产生的相关信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

在您使用本应用进行前庭性眩晕康复训练时，我们可能会收集以下信息：

  • 前置 TrueDepth 摄像头采集的眼动、头动及训练过程相关数据。
  • 训练完成情况、训练表现、训练记录及恢复进度相关数据。
  • 问卷填写结果、症状评估结果及训练安排信息。
  • 应用为识别训练记录生成的用户标识（User ID）。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

我们收集和处理上述信息，仅用于以下目的：

  • 提供前庭性眩晕康复训练功能。
  • 评估您的训练表现、康复进度和当前状况。
  • 生成训练记录并支持后续康复效果分析。
  • 用于模型构建、优化和后续用户康复进度评估。
  • 改进本应用的训练方案、评估能力和产品体验。

3. 摄像头与面部追踪信息

本应用使用设备前置 TrueDepth 摄像头进行眼动和头动追踪，以支持训练与评估功能。相关数据仅用于康复训练、进度评估和模型分析，不用于广告投放、用户画像、跨应用追踪或与康复训练无关的用途。

4. 数据上传与存储

您在训练和评估过程中产生的部分数据可能会上传至我们的服务器，用于训练记录存储、恢复评估及模型分析。我们会尽合理努力保护这些数据的安全，并仅在实现上述功能所必需的范围内使用这些数据。

5. 我们不会如何使用您的数据

  • 我们不会将您的数据用于广告投放。
  • 我们不会将您的数据用于跨应用或跨网站跟踪。
  • 我们不会将您的训练数据用于与本应用康复功能无关的商业用途。

6. 数据共享

除为实现本应用核心功能所必需的服务器处理外，我们不会向无关第三方出售、出租或随意披露您的个人数据。若法律法规另有要求，或为履行法定义务，我们可能在法律允许范围内披露必要信息。

7. 数据安全

我们重视您的数据安全，并采取合理的技术和管理措施保护数据免遭未经授权的访问、使用或泄露。但请您理解，任何互联网传输和电子存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。

8. 您的权利

如果您希望了解、更正或删除与您相关的数据，或对本隐私政策有任何疑问，您可以通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

English

Gaze (the “App”) values your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, and protects information generated during your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App for vestibular dizziness rehabilitation training, we may collect:

  • Eye-movement, head-movement, and training-session data captured through the front TrueDepth camera.
  • Training performance, completion records, and recovery progress data.
  • Questionnaire responses, symptom assessment results, and training plan information.
  • A generated user identifier (User ID) used to associate training records.

2. How We Use This Information

We collect and process this information only for the following purposes:

  • To provide vestibular rehabilitation training functions.
  • To evaluate your training performance, recovery progress, and current condition.
  • To generate training records and support rehabilitation-effect analysis.
  • To support model development, optimization, and future recovery assessment.
  • To improve the App’s training plans, evaluation capabilities, and user experience.

3. Camera and Face-Tracking Data

The App uses the device’s front TrueDepth camera to track eye and head movement for training and assessment features. This data is used only for rehabilitation training, progress evaluation, and model analysis. It is not used for advertising, profiling, cross-app tracking, or any unrelated purpose.

4. Data Upload and Storage

Some data generated during training and assessment may be uploaded to our server for storage, recovery evaluation, and model analysis. We make reasonable efforts to protect this data and use it only to the extent necessary to provide the functions described above.

5. What We Do Not Do With Your Data

  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not use your data for cross-app or cross-website tracking.
  • We do not use your training data for commercial purposes unrelated to rehabilitation.

6. Data Sharing

Except for server-side processing necessary to provide the App’s core features, we do not sell, rent, or arbitrarily disclose your personal data to unrelated third parties. We may disclose necessary information where required by applicable law or legal obligation.

7. Data Security

We value data security and take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Rights

If you would like to access, correct, or delete data related to you, or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us using the details below.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to feature changes, legal requirements, or service improvements. The updated version will be published in an appropriate location.
